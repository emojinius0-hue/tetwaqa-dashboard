emojinius0-hue

emojinius0-hue is a content publishing tool. It lets an account owner connect their own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts through each platform's official authorization (OAuth) and then upload and schedule their own original short and long videos to those accounts from one place.

How it works

  • The account owner authorizes the app on each platform and can revoke access at any time from that platform's security settings.
  • Videos are uploaded only to accounts the owner has authorized, with the title, caption and schedule the owner chose.
  • The app uses each platform's official publishing APIs, including TikTok's Content Posting API.

Connect your TikTok account

Authorize emojinius0-hue to publish videos to your TikTok account (TikTok Login Kit). You will review the requested permissions on TikTok's consent screen and can revoke access at any time.

Connect TikTok

Permissions requested

  • user.info.basic — shows which account was connected (name and avatar).
  • video.publish — publishes the videos you choose to your account (Content Posting API, Direct Post).
  • video.upload — uploads the video file for posting.

Publishing flow

  1. Connect your TikTok account with the button above.
  2. TikTok returns a one-time authorization code to this site.
  3. The app exchanges the code for an access token and uses it to upload and post your video through the Content Posting API.
  4. The video appears on your TikTok profile.

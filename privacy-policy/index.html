Privacy Policy — emojinius0-hue

Last updated: November 2026

emojinius0-hue ("the app") is a content publishing tool that lets an account owner publish and schedule their own videos to their YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts.

Data we use

  • Access tokens issued by YouTube, Instagram and TikTok when the account owner authorizes the app. They are stored privately on the owner's server and are never published or shared.
  • Basic account information (display name and account ID) needed to publish to the authorized account.
  • Public performance numbers of the account's own posts (views, likes, comments), used only to show the owner their statistics.
  • The videos, titles and captions the owner chooses to publish.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect data about viewers or visitors.
  • We do not sell data or share it with third parties.
  • We do not use data for advertising or to track individuals.

Retention, deletion and control

Tokens are kept only while the authorization is active. The account owner can revoke the app's access at any time in the security settings of YouTube, Instagram or TikTok; on revocation the stored token is deleted. To request deletion of any stored data, contact us below.
